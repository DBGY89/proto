Modal “From idea to prototype” — 5 jerarquías (redimensiona para desktop/mobile)

Mockup 1 — Títulos mayores, mismo color

What problem & for whom

For whom: Anyone who needs 30 seconds of something simple.

This: Pop bubbles before they float away. Special ones burst into glitter. That's it.

The iteration

Bubbles was my first prototype. Idea, prompt, working result almost immediately. When an idea is truly simple, the build is simple too.

What AI could and couldn't do

The special bubbles were generating at the bottom and never rising. They existed, just invisible.
