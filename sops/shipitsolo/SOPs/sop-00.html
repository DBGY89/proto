Block 00 — Before you start 00

What can you actually build with this stack?

A lot. And faster than you think. This SOP shows you what is possible before you write a single line of code.

You probably have things you want to do but never find the time for. Train more. Cook better. Learn something new. Spend more time with the people you care about. The tools in this course will not do those things for you. But they will free up the time and energy you need to actually do them.

Every project you build here is a tool that works for you while you are doing something else.

what you will build in this course

Every SOP in this bundle has a task. The tasks connect. By the time you finish the mandatory SOPs, you will have built and shipped a real personal landing page. Not a tutorial exercise. Something live on the internet with your name and a URL you can share.

The landing page is not the point. It is the training ground. Once you know how to build and deploy one project, you know how to build and deploy anything. The next one will take half the time. The one after that will take even less.

see what is possible before you start

Before you write your PRD, try this. Open Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI assistant and paste this prompt:

Your first prompt — try it right now
Build me a prototype for an animal shelter. The shelter rescues dogs and cats in [your city]. The page should feel warm, emotional, and trustworthy. Include: a hero section with a strong headline, three animals available for adoption with their names and personalities, how the adoption process works in three steps, and a contact form. Show me a preview of how it would look.

Look at what comes back. Something that looks real appeared in seconds from a description in plain language. No code. No technical knowledge. That is what you are going to learn to build and put on the internet.

Your task this week SOP 00
Describe your landing page in one paragraph.

Answer three questions in writing: What is your name and what do you do? Who is this page for? What do you want someone to do after visiting it? Write it as a single paragraph. Just make it honest.

When done A written paragraph describing your landing page. Ready to turn into a PRD in SOP 01.
