Block 03 — Final SOP 06

You made it.

You started this course without knowing how to put something online. You just finished it.

Six SOPs ago you had an idea and a blank page. Now you have the tools, the process, and the experience of having built something and made it visible on the internet. That is not a small thing.

Here is everything you can do now.

what you learned
  • SOP 01
    Describe what you want to build so an AI can actually help you
    Not a vague idea, but a document with enough detail that the work can start. You know what to include, what to leave out, and how to get from an idea to a brief that works.
  • SOP 02
    Have a real conversation with an AI coding assistant
    You know how to give it context, how to guide it when it goes in the wrong direction, and how to go from a first rough version to something you actually want to show people.
  • SOP 03
    Take a project from your computer and put it online
    You know how to use GitHub to save your work and GitHub Pages to make it visible. Anyone with the link can see what you built.
  • SOP 04
    Give your site a real address and keep it updated automatically
    You know how to buy a domain and connect it through Vercel so your site lives at an address that belongs to you, and updates on its own every time you make a change.
  • SOP 05
    Let people contact you through your site
    You know how to add a form that saves information into a database. Every submission goes straight to you. No third-party service, no monthly fee.
what this looks like in practice
Marta's story, complete

Marta was the example we used throughout this course. A photographer who needed a simple website to show her work and let clients reach her.

Following these steps, we built her a site where she can show her portfolio and give potential clients a way to hire her. That is just one example. You can build many more, with your own hands. All you need is an idea and the steps in this guide.

Now go build something.

You have everything you need. A site, a domain, a way for people to reach you, and a process for when things go wrong. The only thing left is to use it.
