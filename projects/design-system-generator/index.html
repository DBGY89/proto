From idea to prototype

Built in focused sessions.

What problem and for whom

For whom: Anyone who needs a coherent design system without starting from a blank Figma file.

Problem: Brand colors and tokens scatter across repos. Preview and export should live in one place.

This: You pick colors, a typeface, and a stack. We generate tokens, component previews, and downloadable files.

The iteration

The first version was a single HTML page so the loop stayed fast: tweak inputs, see the preview, copy or download. The lab shell came later so it feels like the rest of AI Proto Lab.

What AI could and could not do

AI helped explore layout and token naming. It does not replace your judgment on contrast and brand. You still pick the three colors that matter.

Minimum viable effort

Three colors, one font, one stack. Generate. Export. Done.
