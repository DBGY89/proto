From idea to prototype
Built in three evenings.
What problem & for whom

For whom: Anyone who has ever wondered why pandas keep showing up in other countries — and suspected it wasn't just about conservation.

Problem: Panda diplomacy is one of China's most documented soft power strategies, and almost nobody knows it by name. The data is public. The stories are wild. Nobody had put them on a globe.

This: A spy-dashboard interface for tracking panda deployments. Click an agent. Read their file. Understand the geopolitics behind the bamboo.

The iteration

The first version was a plain list. It worked but missed the point entirely: the fun of this project is the absurdity of treating pandas as intelligence assets. The dashboard format — with code names, threat levels, alliance indexes and a live globe — turns real diplomatic data into something people actually want to explore.

The briefing screen was added last. Without it, you land cold on a spy UI. With it, the tone is set before you even see a panda.

What AI could and couldn't do

AI was fast at generating the agent profiles: mission, behavior, context, the quote. The humor required more back-and-forth than expected. Getting the tone right — absurd but grounded in real facts — took iteration. The globe and Three.js setup was AI-assisted but needed manual tuning to place markers correctly and handle mobile gracefully.

The hardest part: deciding what the threat level of a panda actually means. That I had to figure out alone.

Minimum viable effort

One globe. Twenty agents. One dossier per agent. The whole thing works in a single HTML file with no backend. The data is static but the experience feels live. That is the whole trick.
