Part of AI Proto Lab. Play. Learn. Repeat.

From idea to prototype

Built in a few evenings.

What problem & for whom

For whom: Anyone who wants to learn simple melodies on the piano without reading sheet music first. Kids, beginners, or people who learn by ear.

Problem: You want to play a tune but don't know which keys to press or how to keep time. Apps either assume you read music or feel like games with no real feedback.

This: Pick a song. We show you the notes in a row and light up the next key. You play it. We tell you if you're right. One flow, your tempo, no theory required.

The iteration

First version had too many modes. I simplified to one screen: you pick a song and choose guided or free. Guided mode lights up the next key and checks your playing; free mode is just the keyboard and partitura for reference. Tempo and metronome stay visible but don't get in the way.

What AI could and couldn't do

AI helped with the song data and note sequences. The timing logic, the Web Audio engine, and the feedback loop (correct vs wrong, streak, score) were built by hand. Getting the feel right (when to advance, when to show "wrong") took more tweaking than the melodies.

Minimum viable effort

One song choice. One Start button. Follow the lit key and the partitura. Record if you want a take. No account, no levels. If you can tap a key, you can use it.
