Dressing recipe

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Combine oil, vinegar, garlic, and oregano in a small bowl.
  2. Whisk and season with salt and pepper. Let sit 5 minutes.
Chef's trick

Restaurant secret: salt goes in the dressing, not on the leaves. Try it once.
