How Hangul Works

Before you start

Korean is written in syllable blocks, not in a line of individual letters. Each block is one syllable, made of 2 or 3 characters stacked together.

This is what makes it different from the alphabet you already know. Once it clicks, it's one of the most logical writing systems in the world.

Two blocks together make a word. 한 + 국 = 한국, han-guk, Korea.

In Level 1 you'll learn the vowels first. Since vowels can't stand alone in a block, Korean uses a silent placeholder consonant: ㅇ. So 아 is just ㅇ + ㅏ, pronounced "a".

Don't worry. It will make sense as soon as you see it in action.
